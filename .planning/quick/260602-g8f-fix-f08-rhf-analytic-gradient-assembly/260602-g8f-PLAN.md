## Discovery that reframed the task

The audit classified F-08 manual-only / multi-wave on the premise that the grad
integrals were "MISSING from cintx" (or, in pass-8, "evaluate today" but only
the multi-wave assembly remained). **Both were wrong.** Ground-truthing showed:

- The grad integrals now EVALUATE, so the structural assembly runs end-to-end.
- But the RHF analytic gradient was numerically GARBAGE (H2/STO-3G FD mismatch
  0.695 Ha/Bohr, broken molecular symmetry) — three real assembly bugs that were
  never finite-difference-validated (written while integrals were unavailable).
- The FD harness (`verify_fd`) is an in-tree oracle — NO live PySCF needed — so
  this is fully verifiable in-sandbox (the F-06/F-07 "run the oracle" lesson).
