<objective>
**Bind the periodic `Cell` — the first argument of every other PBC binding.**

`pyscf-pbc-gto` is 8,775 lines / 223 public items, rooted at `Cell`
(`src/cell.rs:46`). Two facts shape the work.

**`Cell` Derefs to `Mole`** (`cell.rs:148`), mirroring upstream where `Cell`
extends `MoleBase`. Real scripts call the *molecular* surface on a Cell.
`PyCell` must re-expose what `crates/pyscf-py/src/gto.rs` already binds for
`Mole`; hand-duplicating those getters is the obvious trap.

**`Cell::get_hcore` always refuses** — `src/hcore.rs:181` is an unconditional
`NotYetImplemented { phase: 11 }`. Upstream exposes `cell.get_hcore()`, so a
faithful-looking binding would bind a permanent refusal. The real entry point is
`pyscf_pbc_df::get_hcore(&dyn PeriodicDf, kpts)` (`pyscf-pbc-df/src/fftdf.rs:458`)
and belongs to 20-10.
</objective>
