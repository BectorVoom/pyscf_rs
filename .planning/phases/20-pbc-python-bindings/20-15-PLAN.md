`Kmp2::new(&KScfResult, &dyn PeriodicDf)` (`kmp2.rs:52`). The binding accepts an
already-converged driver from 20-12/20-13 and never re-runs SCF.

route** because upstream's own FFTDF/MDF and GDF/RSDF pairs are `9.22e-4 Ha`
apart on diamond `gth-szv [1,1,2]`. A test that compares a KRCCSD energy without
naming its route measures that split, not this port.
