<objective>
Land the deferred `l ≥ 1` AO-on-grid evaluation + the `GTOval_sph_deriv1` variant in `pyscf-kernels::eval_gto` (Phase 2 D-04 deferral), and wire `pyscf-gto::eval_gto`'s `GTOval_sph_deriv1` dispatch to it (replacing the `NotYetImplemented{phase:4}` stub). Purpose: Unblock ρ-evaluation on real molecules and ∇ρ for GGA. This is the one genuinely-new numerical kernel surface the DFT grid loop needs.
Output: `l ≥ 1` + deriv1 eval_gto, element-wise matching upstream.
</objective>
