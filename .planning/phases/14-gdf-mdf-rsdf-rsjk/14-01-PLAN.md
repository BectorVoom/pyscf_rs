<objective>
The one genuinely new primitive of Phase 14: the 3-centre integral `(μν|P)` over
a **double** lattice sum, plus the auxiliary cell that feeds it.

Port target: `pyscf/pbc/df/incore.py` — `make_auxcell` (:52), `aux_e2` (:73),
`wrap_int3c` (:119), `fill_2c2e` (:144), `estimate_rcut` (:440),
`_conc_locs` (:483) — and `pyscf/pbc/df/df.py:make_modrho_basis` (:64).

`Int3cBuilder` (:159) and `gen_int3c_kernel` (:213) are **not** ported: they are
the `ExtendedMole` supermole driver for a C kernel, and D-PBC-23 declines that
machinery. What is ported is the mathematical content those two produce.
</objective>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 3: incore::auxcell — make_auxcell / make_modrho_basis</name>
  New module `crates/pyscf-pbc-df/src/incore/auxcell.rs`.

  * `make_auxcell(cell, auxbasis) -> Cell` — PORT `incore.py:52-66`. Builds the
    aux `Mole` through the molecular path, then **re-runs the periodic
    estimators**: `auxcell.rcut = pbcgto.estimate_rcut(auxcell)`,
    `ke_cutoff = pbcgto.estimate_ke_cutoff(auxcell)`,
    `mesh = cutoff_to_mesh(a, ke_cutoff)`.
    **The Phase-11 `super_cell` defect is the precedent to fear here**: building
    a periodic cell through the molecular constructor and forgetting the
    periodic post-pass silently produced an all-electron system. Assert
    `auxcell.rcut` is set and non-zero before returning.
  * `make_modrho_basis(cell, auxbasis, drop_eta) -> Cell` — PORT `df.py:64-121`.
    Renormalises every aux shell so that the **monopole** integrates to
    `half_sph_norm = sqrt(0.25/pi)`:
    `int1 = gaussian_int(2l+2, es)`, `s = Σ_p cs[p,i]·int1[p]`,
    `cs ← cs · half_sph_norm / s`. Drops primitives with `es < drop_eta`.
    Recomputes `rcut` per shell via `_estimate_rcut(es, l, |cs|.max(1), cell.precision)`
    and sets `auxcell.rcut = max(rcut)`.
    **This normalisation is not the square-norm convention** and getting it
    wrong is invisible until `auxbar` and the compensating charge disagree —
    which is a 14-02 failure, not a 14-01 one. Test it here (Task 6).
  * Aux basis defaults: reuse `pyscf_df::auxbasis`. `gth-szv` has no entry, so
    the even-tempered fallback fires. Measured target for diamond:
    `auxcell.nbas = 36`, `auxcell.nao = 108`, six exponents per `l ∈ {0,1,2}`
    per atom, `e = 7.6024170048 · 2^-n`, `n = 0..5`. For He-fcc/`sto-3g`:
    `nbas = 9`, `nao = 23`, exps `[66.205029, 13.1717136, 3.1622361, 0.8153622]`
    (s), `[9.612881982, 3.20429397, 1.1643102]` (p), `[7.6739799, 0.9877947]` (d).
    If `pyscf_df::auxbasis` does not reproduce those, EXTEND it — do not fork it.
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 4: incore::int3c — the double lattice sum</name>
  New module `crates/pyscf-pbc-df/src/incore/int3c.rs`.

  * `estimate_rcut(cell, auxcell, precision) -> f64` — PORT `incore.py:440-482`,
    the two-pass Newton refinement, verbatim, including that `cs` is the
    **libcint contraction coefficient** from `_extract_pgto_params(cell, 'min')`
    and NOT `gto_norm` (Phase 13 defect #2 — do not repeat it). Measured
    targets: diamond **17.266040957536866**, He-fcc **9.53235156147295**.
  * `aux_e2(cell, auxcell, intor, aosym, kptij_lst) -> Vec<CTensor>` — the
    driver. Implements

    ```text
    T[ki,kj][μν,P] = Σ_{La} e^{i(ki−kj)·La} Σ_{ΔL} e^{i kj·ΔL} ( μ(0) ν(ΔL) | P(La) )
    ```

    (14-CONTEXT.md derives it). Loop shape, copied from
    `pbc_intor::lattice_sum`:
    - `La` over `get_lattice_Ls(cell, rcut = estimate_rcut(cell, auxcell))`
      — the OUTER loop, because the aux-translated basis is rebuilt per `La`;
    - `ΔL` over the same list, screened by the existing
      `pyscf_pbc_gto::neighborlist::build_neighbor_list` shell-pair mask;
    - inner: `build_image_expanded_triple_basis`, one cintx `SessionRequest`
      per `(ish, jsh, ksh)` triple, F-order block stitched exactly as
      `lattice_sum` does, folded into all `nkptij` outputs before the basis
      drops.
    Support `aosym ∈ {s1, s2}`; `s2` skips `ish < jsh` and packs the lower
    triangle, matching `_guess_shell_ranges`' contract. `comp` is 1 —
    component-leading arity-3 families are Phase 18 and return
    `NotYetImplemented { phase: 18 }`.
  * `fill_2c2e(cell, auxcell, intor, hermi, kpt) -> CTensor` — PORT
    `incore.py:144-157`. This is just `auxcell.pbc_intor("int2c2e", ...)`, so
    the only new work is teaching `pbc_intor::SUPPORTED_INTORS` about
    `int2c2e`. Confirm `int2c2e` is arity-2 in the cintx resolver first; if it
    is not, `fill_2c2e` gets its own two-centre lattice sum here and says so.
  * `conc_locs(ao_loc1, ao_loc2)` — PORT `incore.py:483-489`.

  **Do not build a supermole.** `supmol.nbas` is 1486 on diamond; the whole
  point of the `(La, ΔL)` reduction is that the port never materialises it.
</task>

  <name>Task 6: tests/incore.rs</name>
  Six assertions, tier-1 (no oracle) first:

  1. **`make_modrho_basis` shape and normalisation, no oracle.** `auxcell.nbas`
     and `.nao` equal the measured 36/108 (diamond) and 9/23 (He-fcc); and for
     every `l = 0` shell, `∫ χ_P dr = sqrt(0.25/pi) · (4π)^(1/2)` to 1e-14 —
     that is the monopole convention `auxbar` will assume in 14-02.
  2. **`estimate_rcut`, no oracle.** 17.266040957536866 (diamond) and
     9.53235156147295 (He-fcc) to 1e-12.
  3. **THE oracle-free gate: the supercell identity.** At gamma with
     `kmesh = [1,1,1]`, `aux_e2(cell, auxcell)` must equal the *molecular*
     `int3c2e` of a large enough supercell, folded back onto the unit cell, to
     **1e-12**. Build the supercell with `pyscf_pbc_gto::supercell::build_supcell`
     (and remember Phase 11's defect: it must go through `Cell::build`, not
     `pyscf_gto::build_from`). This is the assertion that pins the sign and index
     conventions with no upstream in the loop.
  4. **Hermiticity, no oracle.** `T[ki,kj][μν,P] == conj(T[kj,ki][νμ,P])` to
     1e-13, for all 8 k-points of diamond 2×2×2.
  5. **`s2` packs `s1`, no oracle.** The `aosym='s2'` output must equal the
     lower triangle of the `s1` output, bit-identically.
  6. **Oracle (`PYSCF_ORACLE_VENV`) — RETIRED AND REPLACED, see below.**
     ~~`incore.aux_e2` and `incore.fill_2c2e` to 1e-11 on both cells.~~
     **Against a CHARGED auxiliary cell the 3-centre lattice sum has no
     screening-independent value** — it diverges with `rcut` (16.11 at 9.53
     Bohr, 34.05 at 14.0, brute-forced inside upstream itself), and upstream's
     own result is that sum minus a P-independent `G = 0` offset. The
     compensated tensor `fuse(j3c)` IS well defined (bit-identical at `rcut`
     x1.0/x1.5/x2.0), so **the 1e-11 oracle gate moves to plan 14-02**.
     What 14-01 gates instead:
     **6a — the ISOLATED-CELL identity.** On a one-image cell (He/`sto-3g`,
     `a = 15 I` Bohr) `aux_e2` matches upstream `incore.aux_e2` to **1e-13**
     (measured **8.88e-16**). Nothing but the algebra, the modrho normalisation
     and the two index orders can move that number.
     **6b — `fill_2c2e` positivity** on the same cell.
     Assert `pyscf.__version__ == "2.12.1"` in both.
     Full evidence: `measurements/README.md` § "Recorded during 14-01".
