AFTDF, which is the builder that converges to FFTDF as its mesh rises.

`_CCMDFBuilder` (:354) — plus `mdf_jk.py` (`density_fit` :35, `get_j_kpts` :69,

`general` :42, `ao2mo_7d` :54).

compose 14-02's builder. Ship `_CCMDFBuilder`; `_RSMDFBuilder` waits for 14-07,
the same split 14-03 made for `_prefer_ccdf`.

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: mdf::_CCMDFBuilder</name>
  The whole of MDF is: the aux basis fits what it can, and the **residual**
  `(μν|P)_exact − (μν|P)_fitted` is carried on a plane-wave mesh. Concretely
  `_CCMDFBuilder` keeps `_CCGDFBuilder`'s real-space `j3c` and changes what the
  reciprocal-space pass adds — `weighted_ft_ao` returns the *full* aux FT rather
  than the model-charge one, and `add_ft_j3c` therefore contributes to the aux
  rows as well as the model-charge rows.
  Port it as a **composition over 14-02's builder**, not a copy: the difference
  is three methods, and duplicating `make_j3c` would make the two drift.
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 3: mdf_jk and mdf_ao2mo</name>

<task type="test">
  <name>Task 5: tests/mdf.rs</name>

  1. **GATE 2 — the mesh ladder, no oracle.** `|E_KRHF(MDF, mesh) −
     E_KRHF(FFTDF, mesh 31)|` over `mesh ∈ {7, 11, 15, 21, 27}`, against the
     pre-recorded upstream ladder in `measurements/mdfladder.out`:

     | MDF mesh | diamond 2×2×2 | He-fcc 2×2×2 |
     |---|---|---|
     | 7 (default) | 1.124e-06 | 1.624e-06 |
     | 11 | 9.938e-09 | **3.730e-09** ← best |
     | 15 | 1.296e-09 | 3.243e-08 |
     | 21 | **3.241e-10** ← best | 3.345e-08 |
     | 27 | 2.575e-09 | 2.965e-08 |

     **The ladder is NOT monotone and a monotone gate would fail a correct
     implementation.** Both systems fall steeply, hit a floor, then bounce —
     diamond bottoms at mesh 21 and is 8× worse at 27; He-fcc bottoms at 11 and
     is ~9× worse from 15 on. Two floors are in play: MDF's own aux-basis fit,
     and the **FFTDF reference's** mesh-31 truncation, past which the
     comparison measures the reference rather than MDF. Phase 13 hit exactly
     this structure in its own Gate 2.
     **State the gate as: falls by ≥3 orders from the default mesh to the
     plateau, then stays within one order of the plateau.** GDF alone, for
     scale, sits at 1.222e-03 / 6.002e-05 — the three-to-six orders MDF buys
     are what make this worth gating at all.
  2. **MDF ⊃ GDF, no oracle.** With the residual mesh forced small enough that
     the plane-wave contribution is negligible, `E_MDF → E_GDF`. This is the
     structural check that the two halves are wired the right way round;
     a sign error in `add_ft_j3c` makes MDF *diverge* from both parents.
  3. **`vj`/`vk` Hermiticity, no oracle**, 1e-13.
  4. **Oracle (`PYSCF_ORACLE_VENV`).** `MDF.get_jk`, `.get_eri` and the
     converged `KRHF` energy vs upstream `MDF` with `_prefer_ccdf = True`, at
     **1e-11** on He-fcc and (with `exclude_dd_block = False`) **1e-11** on
     diamond; ≤ **3e-8** vs upstream's default on diamond.
     Upstream default-route energies for the record: **−10.93087429163887**
     (diamond 2×2×2), **−10.13717373919459** (gamma), **−2.80848673371326**
     (He-fcc).
</task>
