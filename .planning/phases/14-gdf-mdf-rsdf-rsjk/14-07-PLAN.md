<objective>
Range-separated Gaussian density fitting: the route `df.GDF` actually takes by
default (`_prefer_ccdf = False`), and the largest single porting task in the
milestone.

Port target: `pyscf/pbc/df/rsdf_builder.py` (1631 l) — `_RSGDFBuilder` (:59)
and its `build` (:127), `weighted_coulG_LR/SR` (:195/:202), `get_q_cond` (:205),
`get_2c2e` (:248), `outcore_auxe2` (:363), `weighted_ft_ao` (:701),
`gen_j3c_loader` (:735), `add_ft_j3c` (:796), `solve_cderi` (:813);
`_RSNucBuilder` (:1098); `_guess_omega` (:1330), `_estimate_meshz` (:1367),
`_eval_gto` (:1379), `_conj_j2c` (:1394), `_gaussian_int` (:1401),
`_round_off_to_odd_mesh` (:1405), `estimate_rcut` (:1418),
`estimate_ft_rcut` (:1506), `estimate_omega_min` (:1580),
`estimate_ke_cutoff_for_omega` (:1595), `estimate_omega_for_ke_cutoff` (:1606),
`estimate_rs_2c2e_rcut` (:1622) — and `pyscf/pbc/df/rsdf_helper.py` (1348 l).

**Split into three sub-tasks by function group** (§8.6's own note), executed and
committed in this order so a failure localises:

* **7a — the ω machinery.** Every `estimate_*`/`_guess_omega` above, plus
  `weighted_coulG_LR`/`_SR`. Pure functions, all unit-testable against upstream
  with no builder in the loop. **Do 7a completely, with its tests green, before
  writing a line of 7b** — the whole scheme's accuracy is one `ω` away, and a
  wrong `ω` shows up as a plausible-looking 1e-6.
* **7b — `_RSGDFBuilder`.** The overrides listed above, composing 14-02's
  already-shipped `decompose_j2c` / `gen_uniq_kpts_groups` / `make_j3c`.
* **7c — `rsdf_helper` + `_RSNucBuilder`.** The short-range 3-centre driver and
  the nuclear-attraction builder.

**What range separation buys.** GDF's compensating charge fixes the `1/R` tail
by subtracting a smooth Gaussian; RSGDF fixes it by splitting the Coulomb kernel
itself, `1/r = erfc(ωr)/r + erf(ωr)/r`. The short-range half is genuinely
short-ranged, so the real-space lattice sum converges fast; the long-range half
is smooth, so few plane waves resolve it. That is why upstream defaults to it and
why the two routes disagree by up to 4.5e-6 (`measurements/ccdf.py`).
</objective>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 7b: _RSGDFBuilder</name>
  Compose over 14-02. The structural differences from `_CCGDFBuilder`:
  * `has_long_range()` is `omega`-dependent, not `dimension`-dependent;
  * `get_2c2e` builds the metric from the SR real-space `int2c2e` plus the LR
    reciprocal-space part, rather than from the fused compensating cell;
  * `outcore_auxe2` runs against the **plain** `auxcell` with an SR kernel,
    not against a fused cell — so 14-01's `aux_e2` needs an `omega` parameter
    threaded to cintx's range-separated `int3c2e`. **Check first that the
    cintx resolver exposes a short-range `int3c2e`**; if it does not, this is
    the plan's one real blocker and it must be reported as such, not worked
    around with a numerically different kernel.
  * `add_ft_j3c` adds the LR part to ALL aux rows (there are no model-charge
    rows).
  `exclude_dd_block` stays `false` (D-PBC-23) here too.
</task>
