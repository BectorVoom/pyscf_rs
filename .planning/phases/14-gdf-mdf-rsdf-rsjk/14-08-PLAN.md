<objective>
The user-facing `RSDF` class, its `density_fit` shim, and `rsjk` — the
range-separated J/K build that uses **no** density fitting at all.

Port target: `pyscf/pbc/df/rsdf.py` — `get_aux_chg` (:65), `RSGDF` (:75),
`_RSGDFBuilder` (:323, the *subclass* that specialises 14-07's) —
`pyscf/pbc/df/rsdf_jk.py` (`density_fit` :21, 53 l) and
`pyscf/pbc/scf/rsjk.py` (1355 l).

**`rsjk` is a different animal from everything else in this phase.** It builds
`vj`/`vk` directly by splitting the Coulomb operator, with no auxiliary basis
and no `cderi`. It belongs in `pyscf-pbc-scf`, not `pyscf-pbc-df`, and it must
not be given a `PeriodicDf` impl whose `sr_loop`/`get_naoaux` half is a lie.
Give it its own narrow trait or a plain struct with `get_jk`, and wire it as an
alternative `get_veff` source.
</objective>

<task type="test">
  <name>Task 5: tests/rsdf.rs and tests/rsjk.rs</name>

  1. **`get_aux_chg` == 14-01's monopole, no oracle**, 1e-14.
  2. **GATE 3 (no oracle, then oracle).** `|E_KRHF(GDF) − E_KRHF(RSDF)|` in the
     port must sit on upstream's measured floor:
     **1.353e-08** (diamond 2×2×2), **4.566e-09** (gamma),
     **1.113e-10** (He-fcc 2×2×2), each within a factor of 2.
     Then the oracle arm: `RSDF`'s converged energy vs upstream at **1e-11**
     (He-fcc, target **−2.80842508705964**) and ≤ **3e-8** (diamond,
     targets **−10.93209530458920** / **−10.14369691810517**).
  3. **`rsjk` vs FFTDF, no oracle.** `rsjk` builds the *exact* J/K — no fitting
     — so its `vj`/`vk` must match `FFTDF`'s to the mesh/lattice-sum floor, and
     its `KRHF` energy must match FFTDF's, **not** GDF's. Gate at the
     FFTDF−AFTDF floor Phase 13 established: **2.607e-11** (diamond 2×2×2),
     **3.006e-13** (He-fcc). Getting `rsjk` gated against a *fitted* builder
     would hide a real error behind the 1.2e-3 fitting gap — that mistake is
     the single most likely way this plan ships broken.
  4. **`rsjk` Hermiticity and `exxdiv`, no oracle**, as 14-04 Task 5.
  5. **Oracle.** `rsjk.get_jk` and the converged `KRHF` energy vs upstream at
     **1e-11** on both systems.
  6. **`df_swap.rs`:** all six builders — FFTDF, AFTDF, GDF, MDF, RSDF and the
     `rsjk` route — drive `KRHF` with no driver change, and FFTDF stays
     bit-identical.
</task>
