<objective>
Make the MO transform reachable through `Box<dyn PeriodicDf>`, and build the
`Lov` tensor KMP2's DF route runs on.

`kmp2.kernel:92` is `fao2mo = mp._scf.with_df.ao2mo`. In this port the mean
field owns a `Box<dyn PeriodicDf>` (`krhf.rs:29`, `kuhf.rs:31`) and that trait
has `get_nuc` / `get_pp` / `get_jk` and **no ao2mo at all**
(`traits.rs:66-100`). The integrals themselves are all shipped — plans 13-06
and 14-05 landed `general` / `get_eri` / `get_mo_pairs_g` / `ao2mo_7d` for
AFTDF, FFTDF, GDF and MDF. What is missing is the seam, plus RSDF, plus
`_init_mp_df_eris`, plus the legacy `pbc/ao2mo` package.

**The index contract is already settled** (`df_ao2mo.rs:34-70`:
`eri[ki,kj,kk][i,j,k,l]`, `kl = kconserv[ki,kj,kk]`, chemists' notation,
asserted in `tests/df_ao2mo.rs`). Phase 13 deliberately refused to guess it and
14-05 settled it against all three upstream files. **Nothing here re-derives,
re-orders, or "improves" it.**
</objective>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 3: crates/pyscf-pbc-mp/src/lov.rs — `_init_mp_df_eris`</name>
  PORT `pyscf/pbc/mp/kmp2.py:156-227`.

  ```
  Lov[ki,kj][L, i, a] = Σ_pq conj(C_ki[p,i]) · cderi[ki,kj][L,pq] · C_kj[q,a]
  ```

  with `i` over the **occupied** columns of `mo_coeff[ki]` and `a` over the
  **virtual** columns of `mo_coeff[kj]`. Upstream reaches that by stacking
  `mo = hstack((mo_coeff[ki], mo_coeff[kj]))` and slicing
  `(0, nocc, nmo+nocc, nmo+nocc+nvir)` — the stacking is an artefact of
  `_ao2mo.r_e2`'s C signature and **this port does not need it**: call
  `df_ao2mo::r_e2(blk, nao, &occ_slice_i, &vir_slice_j)` directly, using
  `moref::mo_slice` from plan 15-03. Say so in a `DEVIATION:` comment, with the
  upstream line, and prove it in Task 5 test 2.

  * Input: anything with `sr_loop` + `get_naoaux` — take
    `&dyn PeriodicDf` and require `has_cderi()`, erroring otherwise with the
    name of the builder, rather than silently producing zeros.
  * `cell.dimension == 2` → **error**, PORT `:145-153`. Upstream raises
    `NotImplementedError` because the negative-part `cderi` block is unhandled;
    dropping it silently is a wrong number, not a missing feature.
  * The `sr_loop` sign: every yield carries `sign ∈ {+1, −1}` (the 2-D negative
    block). Since 2-D is refused, assert `sign == 1` rather than ignoring it.
  * Rank: `get_naoaux` may differ per k-difference on the eigen route — 14-05's
    defect (3) was a `get_naoaux` **stricter** than upstream. `Lov[ki,kj]`
    therefore carries its own `naux`, and nothing may assume it is uniform.
  * Shape: return `LovTable { nkpts, nocc, nvir, blocks: Vec<(usize, CTensor)> }`
    where the `usize` is that block's `naux`, indexed `[ki*nkpts + kj]`.

  **Speed (D-PBC-27, 15-CONTEXT §7):** every `(ki, kj)` block is independent —
  `Lov[ki,kj]` reads only `cderi[ki,kj]` and the occupied/virtual slices of
  `mo_coeff[ki]`/`mo_coeff[kj]` — so build the `nkpts²` blocks with
  `rayon::par_iter` over the flat `[ki*nkpts + kj]` index, writing each result
  into its own disjoint slot of `LovTable::blocks`. There is no shared
  accumulator here (unlike the `emp2` sum in 15-05), so no `oracle_sum` is
  needed — only a `RAYON_NUM_THREADS` 1-vs-8 bit-identity test proving the
  per-block writes really are disjoint in code, not merely on paper.
</task>

<task type="test">
  <name>Task 5: tests</name>

  **crates/pyscf-pbc-df/tests/ao2mo_dispatch.rs** — tier-1, no oracle.

  1. **The trait reaches the same numbers as the free function.** For each of
     `Fftdf`, `Aftdf`, `Gdf`, `Mdf`, `Rsdf` on `he_fcc` `sto-3g` 2×2×2:
     `(&df as &dyn PeriodicDf).ao2mo(...)` equals the concrete
     `general(...)` **bit-for-bit** (`assert_eq!` on the planes), same for
     `ao2mo_7d`. A delegation that reaches the wrong builder is otherwise a
     plausible-looking wrong number.
  2. **`Rsdf` equals its inner `Gdf` with `prefer_ccdf = false`.** Bit-for-bit,
     both methods. This is what "one fitting pipeline" means, stated as a test.
  3. **`has_cderi` matches upstream's `isinstance(..., df.GDF)`.** Five
     implementors, five expected values. `kmp2.py:69` branches the entire
     energy route on it.
  4. **The contract survives.** Re-assert, here and not only in
     `tests/df_ao2mo.rs`, that `ao2mo_7d`'s `[ki,kj,kk]` block equals
     `general` at the quadruple `[ki,kj,kk,kconserv[ki,kj,kk]]` — for at least
     two implementors, one with `cderi` and one without.

  **crates/pyscf-pbc-mp/tests/lov.rs**

  5. **`Lov` reproduces `(ia|jb)`, no oracle.** The identity that defines it:
     `Σ_L Lov[ki,ka][L,i,a] · Lov[kj,kb][L,j,b]` (no conjugate — see
     `df_ao2mo.rs:12-19` for why) equals `nkpts ·` the `oovv` block that
     `Gdf::general` produces at `[ki,ka,kj,kb]` with the same sliced MO blocks,
     `kb = kconserv[ki,ka,kj]`. Tolerance 1e-12; both sides are the same
     contraction associated differently.
  6. **The un-stacked slice equals the stacked slice.** Build `Lov` once through
     `mo_slice` and once through an explicit `hstack`-then-slice mimicking
     `kmp2.py:180-190`, and assert bit-identity. This is what licenses the
     documented deviation in Task 3.
  7. **2-D is refused, not approximated.** A `dimension = 2` cell returns the
     error.
  8. **Per-k `naux` may differ.** On an MDF/eigen-route object where the ranks
     legitimately differ per k-difference (14-05 measured 10 vs 11 at mesh 15),
     `Lov` builds without error and each block carries its own `naux`.
  9. **Oracle (`PYSCF_ORACLE_VENV`), `#[ignore]`d.** `Lov[ki,kj]` matches
     `kmp2._init_mp_df_eris` element-wise on `he_fcc` 2×2×2, at the tolerance
     15-01 Task 6 measured. Assert `pyscf.__version__ == "2.12.1"`.
  9b. **Rayon determinism (D-PBC-27).** `LovTable` built at
      `RAYON_NUM_THREADS = 1` and `= 8` is bit-identical, block for block, on
      `diamond [2,2,2]`.

  **crates/pyscf-pbc-ao2mo/tests/eris.rs**

  10. `get_ao_eri(cell, kpts)` equals `Fftdf::get_ao_eri` at the same
      quadruple, bit-for-bit — the wrapper adds no arithmetic.
  11. At gamma with real coefficients the result is real to 1e-14 and carries
      the molecular 8-fold symmetry.
