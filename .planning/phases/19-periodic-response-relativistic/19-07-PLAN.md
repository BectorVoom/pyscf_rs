<objective>
**The method the phase gate names.**

`pbc/tdscf/krhf.py` (537 l). `ROADMAP:465`'s gate is the KRHF-TDA lowest
excitation, so this plan carries the headline number - now at 19-01's measured
floor rather than the impossible `1e-15 eV`.

The structural difference from 19-06 is the transferred momentum: the response
matrix is block-diagonal in the k-shift, so roots belong to a shift. **Report
them with their shift and sort within it, never across** - a global sort mixes
shifts and produces a number that happens to be close on symmetric cells and
wrong elsewhere.
</objective>
