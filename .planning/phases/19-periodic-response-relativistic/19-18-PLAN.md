<objective>
**Three small, independent modules - 835 lines with no dependency on the rest
of the phase.**

`x2c/sfx2c1e.py` (355), `x2c/x2c1e.py` (286), `eph/eph_fd.py` (181). They are
in one plan because each is independent of every other plan in the phase and
none is large enough to stand alone - not because they are conceptually related.

Two different gate characters here, and conflating them would be the error:
**X2C is the tightest family in the phase** (upstream asserts 8 decimals in 29
of 34 assertions) precisely because it is a one-electron transformation with no
iterative solver and therefore no convergence noise. **`eph_fd` is a finite
difference**, so its floor is a cancellation floor of the kind 18-01 measures -
it can never be as tight, and must not inherit X2C's number.
</objective>
