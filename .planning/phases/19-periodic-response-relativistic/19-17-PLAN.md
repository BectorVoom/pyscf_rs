<objective>
**The DF variant, and Gate D closed for the family.**

`pbc/adc/dfadc.py` is only 62 lines - a density-fitted variant over the
machinery 19-14 through 19-16 built.

The reason it is its own plan is the **gate**, not the code. Phase 14 measured
the DF fitting error at `1.222e-03 Ha` and Phase 16 found upstream's own
FFTDF/MDF and GDF/RSDF pairs `9.22e-4 Ha` apart. A DF route and a conventional
route are different approximations; each is gated against **its own** upstream
number. This plan states that and rolls up Gate D for IP, EA and DF together.
</objective>
