<objective>
**The smallest complete method in the phase, done first so its shape is settled.**

`pbc/tdscf/rhf.py` (238 l) - gamma-point TDA and TDHF. Every later tdscf plan
(19-07 k-points, 19-08 unrestricted, 19-09 the KS subclasses) copies this
structure, so it is worth getting the driver/solver/root-extraction split right
here rather than four times.

**Root ordering is the trap.** A Davidson solve returns roots in whatever order
it converges them. Compare **sorted** eigenvalues with an explicit `nroots`,
never positionally - and remember upstream's own `nroots` spread reached
`5.11e-7` in Phase 16, which bounds any gate.
</objective>
