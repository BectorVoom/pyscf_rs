<objective>
**The unrestricted pair, ported together because they share a structure.**

`pbc/tdscf/uhf.py` (268 l) and `pbc/tdscf/kuhf.py` (540 l). Kept in one plan
because the k-generalisation of the unrestricted response is the same step
19-07 made for the restricted one - splitting them would write the shared half
twice.

The trap is the coupling: an unrestricted response is **not** two independent
restricted responses. The alpha and beta rotation spaces couple through the
Coulomb kernel, and a driver that solves them separately produces plausible
numbers that are wrong.
</objective>
