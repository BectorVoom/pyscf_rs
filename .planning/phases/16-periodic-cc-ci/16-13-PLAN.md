<objective>
Port `pyscf/pbc/ci/kcis_rhf.py` (700 l) — the first half of `§8.8`'s plan
16-09. **The second half does not ship, and this plan records why rather than
leaving a gap** (`16-CONTEXT §1.6`).

`§8.8` pairs "`KCIS` + `pbc/ci/cisd`". They are unrelated:

* `kcis_rhf.py` is a real **k-point CIS** with its own Davidson (`:97`) and a
  dense fallback (`:113`).
* `pbc/ci/cisd.py` (116 l) is a **Γ-only shim** — `RCISD.__init__` is
  `if abs(mf.kpt).max() > 1e-9: raise NotImplementedError` (`:24`), same at
  `:47` — over molecular `cisd.RCISD` / `ucisd.UCISD` / `gcisd` (`:18`).
  **This port has no molecular CI crate at all**: there is no
  `crates/pyscf-ci`, and molecular CISD is not in any phase of `ROADMAP.md`.
  Porting it means first porting molecular RCISD/UCISD/GCISD, which is a phase,
  not a task.
</objective>
