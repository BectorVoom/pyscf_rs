<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: the narrow molecular `eom_rccsd` surface</name>
  `crates/pyscf-ccsd/src/eom.rs` — the `EOM` / `EOMIP` / `EOMEA` base-class
  shape only: `kernel`, `get_init_guess`, `gen_matvec`, and the
  `nroots` / `koopmans` / `partition` surface. **Not** the molecular matvecs
  (`eom_kccsd_ghf.py` replaces every one) and not the 2109-line module.

  Module doc states this is a partial port serving `pbc/cc/eom_kccsd_*`, lists
  what is deliberately absent and names the phase that would complete it.
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: `EOMIP` / `EOMEA` / `EOMEE` at k, and the Davidson driver</name>
  `eom_kccsd_ghf.py:684` (`EOMIP`), `:760` (`EOMIP_Ta`), `:1201` (`EOMEA`),
  `:1277` (`EOMEA_Ta`), `:1691` (`EOMEE`).

  The two `davidson_nosym1` call sites (`:128`, `:1352`) drive 16-03's solver
  with a `pickeig` closure (`:130-136`). **The matvec is matrix-free** — the
  EOM Hamiltonian is `(nkpts·nocc·nvir)²`-shaped and forming it is what the
  iterative solver exists to avoid. `16-REVIEW.md §4.1` gives the byte counts
  and shows the Davidson **subspace** (`max_space × vector_size`), not the
  Hamiltonian, is the real allocation to bound.

  `kshift` is a first-class loop index here, not a parameter with a default:
  the k-point EOM solves one Davidson per shift.
</task>

<task type="auto">

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 4: tests</name>
  1. **Koopmans limit, no oracle** — with `t1 = t2 = 0` the EOM-IP/EA roots
     reduce to the negatives of the occupied / the virtual orbital energies.
     Exact, oracle-free, and it catches a sign error, a wrong `kshift` and a
     transposed matvec in one assertion. Written first.
  2. **matvec linearity and matrix-freeness** — `H(αx + βy) = αH(x) + βH(y)`
     to 1e-12, and an allocation assertion that no `vector_size²` buffer is
     ever reserved.
  3. **IP + EA vs a dense reference** on the smallest fixture: build the
     Hamiltonian explicitly by applying the matvec to unit vectors, diagonalise
     it densely, and compare against the Davidson roots to 1e-10. Oracle-free,
     and it is the only test that gates the Davidson and the matvec
     independently.
  4. Roots vs upstream (`test_eom_kgccsd.py`, `test_eom_kgccsd_diag.py`) at
     16-01 Task 4's measured tolerance — upstream's own suite asserts these at
     **3 decimals** (`16-CONTEXT §2`).
  5. Determinism (§9.3) — bit-identical roots at `RAYON_NUM_THREADS` 1 and 8.
  6. A peak-memory assertion against `16-REVIEW.md §4.1`'s bound.
</task>
