    coincide, and that is the case this plan ships — **state the restriction in
    the type doc and refuse the complex-Γ case explicitly** rather than let it
    silently take the real path. `mbpt2=True` delegates to Phase 15's
    `RMP2`/`UMP2`/`GMP2` (`:29`, `:66`, `:100`).
</task>
