<task type="tdd">
  <name>Task 1: kmp2_ksymm.kernel over IBZ k-tuples</name>
  `kmp2_ksymm.py:30-119` (`kernel`) and `:120-128` (`kernel_with_t2`).

  This is a rewrite of `kmp2.kernel`'s loop structure, not a wrapper: the
  `(ki, ka, kj)` triple loop runs over `make_ktuples_ibz(ntuple=3)` (17-05
  Task 5) and each surviving block is unfolded through `KsymmArray`'s
  `transform_4d` (17-06 Task 3).

  Everything 15-CONTEXT §3 recorded about `kmp2.kernel` still binds, and this
  plan inherits rather than re-decides it:

  * **`LARGE_DENOM = 1e14` is load-bearing arithmetic, not a guard.** Padded
    orbitals must contribute `~1e-28`; skipping them is a different program.
  * **`kernel`'s two separate `ka` loops are not a style choice** — the exchange
    term at `ka` reads the block at `kb`, so a fused loop is wrong for every
    `ka` with `kb > ka`. Under k-symmetry the loop bounds change; **the read/write
    ordering constraint does not.** Re-derive the fusion boundary for the IBZ
    tuple set explicitly and write down why it is where it is.
  * `1/nkpts` — still the full-BZ `nkpts`, never `nkpts_ibz`; the IBZ weights
    enter through the k-tuple multiplicity, not the prefactor.
  * The `nkpts³·nocc²·nvir²` accumulation is a D-PBC-17 shape and goes through
    `oracle_sum`/`oracle_zsum` from the first version.

  **Speed:** the IBZ k-tuple set is exactly the point of doing this over
  `make_ktuples_ibz` instead of the full `nkpts³` — parallelise the outer
  `(ki, ka)` loop with rayon and reduce each tuple's contribution through
  `oracle_sum`/`oracle_zsum` so the `RAYON_NUM_THREADS` 1-vs-8 bit-identity gate
  (already required below) is a property of the code, not an accident of a
  single-threaded first draft.
</task>

<task type="tdd">
  <name>Task 3: the gate is TIGHTER than the SCF's, and that is deliberate</name>
  17-CONTEXT §2.3. Upstream gates `KMP2` ksymm vs full BZ at **10 decimals**
  (`mp/test/test_ksym.py:56`) and `rdm1` at 10 (`:66`), while gating the *SCF*
  comparison at 7. The reason is structural: MP2 reads **one** converged SCF's
  orbitals through two index paths, so there is no second convergence to add
  noise. **Do not relax this to the SCF's 5e-8 by analogy** — that would let a
  real index-map defect through.

  Gates, at 17-01 Task 4's measured numbers:
  * `|e_corr(ksymm) − e_corr(full BZ)|` on the port's own two runs, He (upstream's
    `test_ksym.py` cell) and `si [2,2,2]`.
  * `|e_corr(port) − e_corr(upstream)|`, **per DF route** — `kmp2.py:69`
    branches the whole `(ia|jb)` computation on `isinstance(with_df, df.GDF)`, so
    upstream computes KMP2 two different ways and an unqualified assertion is
    untestable (15-CONTEXT §2).
  * `Tr(rdm1)` and Hermiticity.
  * §9.3: `e_corr` and `t2` bit-identical at `RAYON_NUM_THREADS` 1 and 8.
  * **Cost, reported not gated:** IBZ vs full-BZ wall time and peak memory.
</task>
