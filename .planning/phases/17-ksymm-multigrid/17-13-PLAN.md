three planning files that 17-01 rewrote so they still agree at the end.

are why Phase 14's close survived a reopened gate two days later without anyone

    816 / 2052 on upstream's Si cell at `[16,16,16]`, plus the same six for each

    separately. Note upstream gates DFT-on-GDF tighter (8 decimals) than

    quadrature floor — explicitly **not** at Gates C/D's level, with the reason
    restated in the document.

  and — if Phase 16 landed — `KRCCSD` `e_corr`/`t1`/`t2`.
