<task type="tdd">
  <name>Task 1: _RangeSeparatedCell — the compact/smooth split</name>
  `ft_ao.py:253-564`. `from_cell` (`:267-399`) decontracts the basis and sorts
  every primitive into compact / smooth / local by exponent against
  `ke_cut_threshold` and `rcut_threshold`; `_reverse_bas_map` (`:400`),
  `smooth_basis_cell` (`:408`), `compact_basis_cell` (`:426`), `recontract`
  (`:477`), `recontract_1d` (`:508`), `get_ao_type` (`:514`),
  `decontract_basis` (`:529`).

  **D-PBC-21 said this machinery is "numerically transparent — it decontracts and
  recontracts, and only *drops* terms below `cell.precision·1e-2`". That claim is
  now testable and this task tests it:**

  * `recontract(decontract(x)) == x` bit-exactly for a random `x`.
  * `ft_aopair` evaluated over the RS cell and recontracted equals the current
    direct-lattice-sum result to 1e-13 **with all screens disabled**. If it does
    not, D-PBC-21's premise was wrong and the phase must know before anything is
    built on it.

  `bas_type = [1]` on He-fcc `sto-3g` (D-PBC-23: "which has no smooth shell") is
  a fixture that must survive — the all-electron control's `exclude_dd_block`
  cost is *exactly 0*, and that zero is what made Phase 14's Gate 1 statable.
</task>

<task type="tdd">
  <name>Task 2: ExtendedMole — the BvK supermole</name>
  `ft_ao.py:565-743`. `from_cell` (`:596-630`) builds the Born–von-Kármán
  supercell over `kmesh`; `sh_loc` (`:585`), `bas_map` (`:590`), `strip_basis`
  (`:631-668`), `get_ovlp_mask` (`:669-704`), `bas_mask_to_segment` (`:705`),
  `bas_type_to_indices` (`:730`), and `estimate_rcut` (`:744-790`).

  **`strip_basis` is where 14-05 found a defect that its own gate could not
  see.** 14-VERIFICATION defect (4): the gate compared against a standalone
  `incore.Int3cBuilder`, "which strips nothing", so the 1.054e-09 was invisible.
  Six measurements localised it — every *input* matched (`weighted_ft_ao`
  6.9e-18, `auxbar` 2.8e-17, `int1e_ovlp` 2.9e-15, `L` 1.1e-13, real-space
  `fuse(j3c)` 1.4e-12) and the assembly did not.

  Two consequences that must be honoured here:

  1. **The comparison must be against upstream's `ExtendedMole`, not against
     this port's unstripped builder.** Repeating 14-05's mistake would make the
     plan's gate blind to the exact quantity it exists to fix.
  2. **The port is currently the MORE converged of the two.** Flattening
     upstream's per-shell-pair radius array to its own maximum collapses the
     difference to 7.333e-13. So `strip_basis` here is a **performance** change
     that must not *lose* accuracy. Gate it both ways: agreement with upstream's
     per-pair radii to ~1e-9, **and** no regression against the current flattened
     result. A `strip_basis` that speeds things up and moves `j3c` by 1e-9 in the
     wrong direction is a regression wearing an optimisation's clothes.
</task>

<task type="tdd">
  <name>Task 3: close exclude_dd_block</name>
  `ft_ao.py:439-476` (`merge_diffused_block`) plus `gdf_builder`'s `_int_dd_block`
  route. Close the refusals at `gdf_builder/mod.rs:96-102`,
  `rsdf_builder/mod.rs:190-196` and the `estimate_rcut` `true` half at
  `gdf_builder/eta.rs:191-196`.

  D-PBC-23 is explicit that this is **not** screening: it "RE-ROUTES the
  smooth–smooth block of `(ij|L)` out of the real-space sum and into an FFT, and
  upstream's default is the *more* accurate route".

  Acceptance targets, already measured by `measurements/ddblock.py`:

  | cell | `|E(dd=false) − E(dd=true)|` |
  |---|---|
  | diamond `gth-szv` 2×2×2 | **1.835e-08 Ha** |
  | diamond gamma | **2.900e-08 Ha** |
  | He-fcc `sto-3g` 2×2×2 | **exactly 0** (no smooth shell) |

  The port must **reproduce those three numbers**, He-fcc's zero included — the
  zero is the strongest of the three, because any implementation that perturbs a
  cell with no smooth basis is wrong by construction.

  Then flip the default to `true`, matching upstream, and — following plan
  14-07 Task 7d's lesson — **pin BOTH routes against their own upstream numbers**.
  14-07 found that flipping `_prefer_ccdf` while pinning only one route would
  have left a test passing while silently measuring the other.
</task>
