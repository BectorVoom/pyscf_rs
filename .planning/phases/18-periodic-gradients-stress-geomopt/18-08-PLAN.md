<objective>
`pyscf/pbc/grad/krkspu.py` (142 l) and `kukspu.py` (83 l) — the DFT+U
gradients. `kukspu` is thin: it imports `generate_first_order_local_orbitals`
straight from `krkspu` (`kukspu.py:24`) and differs only in the spin threading
of `_hubbard_U_deriv1`.

Almost all of the substrate exists. `crates/pyscf-pbc-dft/src/kspu.rs` already
ships `set_u` (`:162`, `rkspu.py:133-169`) and `make_minao_lo` (`:274`,
`krkspu.py:161-176`), and 17-08 shipped `kukspu_ksymm`. What does **not** exist
is the derivative of the local-orbital construction, and that is this plan.
</objective>

<tasks>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: `generate_first_order_local_orbitals`</name>
  Port `krkspu.py:27-88` — the largest and only genuinely new body in this
  plan. It returns a closure giving `∂C_lo/∂R` for the MINAO-referenced local
  orbitals, and the substance is differentiating the **Löwdin/symmetric
  orthogonalisation** that `make_minao_lo` performs: `S^{-1/2}`'s derivative is
  not `-½ S^{-3/2} ∂S`, because `S` and `∂S` do not commute. Upstream solves
  the Sylvester-type relation in the eigenbasis of `S`
  (`krkspu.py:~55-80`, `scipy.linalg` calls at `:21`).

  Two rules:
  * `_set_U`, `_make_minao_lo` and `reference_mol` are **called** from
    `pyscf_pbc_dft::kspu`, never re-implemented. Two local-orbital
    constructions in one workspace is how a plausible wrong number ships
    (`16-CONTEXT §1.1`'s ruling).
  * the overlap derivative it consumes is 18-05's `get_ovlp`
    (`-pbc_intor('int1e_ipovlp')`), with the sign already applied there.

  Gate against upstream's own dedicated test:
  `test_krkspu.py:24 test_finite_diff_local_orbitals` asserts
  `abs(C1[k][1] - ref).max()` to 6 decimals (`:43`) — a *component-level* test
  of exactly this function, independent of any gradient. Run it first; if it
  fails nothing downstream can be trusted.
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: `_hubbard_U_deriv1`, restricted and unrestricted</name>
  Port `krkspu.py:89-134` and `kukspu.py:26-75`. The `U` energy derivative
  contracts the first-order local orbitals against the occupation matrix per
  Hubbard site.

  **17-08's D-17-08-02 applies here and must not be re-litigated**: DFT+U
  rotates **no** projectors under k-point symmetry. That finding was about the
  symmetry adapter, not about the gradient, but it is the same projector set —
  if a rotation appears in this body, it is a bug, not a port.

  Gate against `test_krkspu.py:45 test_finite_diff_hubbard_U_grad` and
  `test_kukspu.py:24`, both asserting `de[1,1]` against
  `(e1 - e2)/2e-3*BOHR` to 6 decimals — note the **`*BOHR`**: those two tests
  displace in Ångström, not Bohr, unlike every other test in the directory.
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 3: the `Gradients` classes and Gates B/C at the DFT+U tier</name>
  `krkspu.Gradients(krks_grad.Gradients)` (`:135`) and
  `kukspu.Gradients(kuks_grad.Gradients)` (`:76`) — 18-07's classes with
  `extra_force` extended by the `U` term.

  **Gate C**: `e` to 8 decimals against `-15.939464667807849` and `lib.fp(g)`
  to **5** decimals against `-0.42370983409650914` (`test_krkspu.py:66-67`,
  `test_kukspu.py:66-67`).

  **Gate B**: `verify_fd` at **5e-6**, not the phase's 1e-6. Upstream's own
  DFT+U assertions are 5-decimal (`test_krkspu.py:87,92`;
  `test_kukspu.py:67,72`) where every other body in the directory is 6-decimal,
  and a blanket 1e-6 would fail against upstream's own published numbers
  (`18-CONTEXT §2.3`, Gate B's second tier). 18-01 measured the actual floor;
  gate at the measurement, and record why this tier is looser rather than
  leaving it looking like an unexplained exception.
</task>
