carries them.**

`PBC-MASTER-PLAN §8.10`'s 18-07 row says **1e-6**; the shipped harness constant

(`test_rks_stress.py:406`).

all four documents in one pass. **A gate that appears in three documents with
three numbers is what this plan exists to end.**

**This plan writes no Rust.**
