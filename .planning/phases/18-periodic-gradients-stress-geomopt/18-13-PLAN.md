<objective>
The three stress modules that derive from 18-12: `uks_stress.py` (246 l),
`krks_stress.py` (404 l) and `kuks_stress.py` (308 l).

None of them re-derives the strain machinery — each imports the same eight
symbols from `rks_stress` (`18-CONTEXT §1.5`). What they add is the spin index
(`uks`, `kuks`), the k index (`krks`, `kuks`), a k-point `get_ovlp`/`get_kin`
pair, and the DFT+U strain term.

**This is the plan where D-PBC-30 clause 1 actually bites.** The gamma block in
18-12 is `(10 + 36) · blk · nao` reals; the k-point block here is the same
count of **complex** numbers **times `nkpts`** — 1.14 GiB per block on diamond
`gth-dzvp` at 2×2×2 (`18-REVIEW §3.1`), on a machine where 17-12's host suite
is SIGKILLed at exit 137.
</objective>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: `uks_stress` — the spin index, gamma</name>
  Port `uks_stress.py:34-245`. It imports the eight symbols from `rks_stress`
  (`:24-33`) and differs from 18-12 only in `get_vxc` (`:34`) and `kernel`
  (`:184`), where `rho0`/`rho1` grow a leading spin axis and `eval_xc_eff` is
  called with `spin = 1`.

  Everything 18-12 ruled applies unchanged: the fused XC contraction, the
  `oracle_sum` accumulator, `rho0` and `rho1[:,:,:,0]` full-grid, the block
  budget counting both AO arrays. The spin axis doubles `rho0`/`rho1` and
  leaves the AO arrays alone — the budget arithmetic in 18-12 Task 2 must
  therefore be parameterised on `nset`, not hard-coded to 1.
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: `krks_stress` — the k index, and its own `get_ovlp`/`get_kin`</name>
  Port `krks_stress.py:84-335`. Two pieces are **not** inherited from
  `rks_stress`:

  * `get_ovlp` (`:84-99`) and `get_kin` (`:100-115`) are k-point versions —
    `cell1.pbc_intor('int1e_ovlp', kpts=kpts)` rather than the gamma form. They
    are 36 lattice sums again, and 18-12 Task 1's single-`Ls` ruling applies
    here identically; `intor_cross_with_images` already takes `kpts`
    (`pbc_intor.rs:295`).
  * `get_vxc` (`:116-263`) evaluates the strain AO for **all k-points at once
    per block** — `_eval_ao_strain_derivatives(cell, coords, kpts, deriv)` at
    `:170` returns `nkpts` of them, and `block_loop(..., kpts=kpts)` at `:170`
    returns `nkpts` ordinary AO tables beside it. `rho0`/`rho1` are accumulated
    with `+=` **across k** (`:183-197`), so they stay k-independent and the
    fused-contraction ruling survives.

  This is where 18-12 Task 2's budget does its real work. Assert the computed
  per-block footprint against `nkpts · 46 · blk · nao · 16` for GGA, and pin a
  low budget in one test so the small-`blk` path runs in CI.
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 3: `kuks_stress` — both indices</name>
  Port `kuks_stress.py:36-307`. Note the import line (`:24`):

  ```python
  from pyscf.pbc.grad.krks_stress import get_ovlp, _get_first_order_local_orbitals
  ```

  — `get_ovlp` comes from **`krks_stress`** (the k-point one), not from
  `rks_stress`, and so does the first-order local-orbital helper. Getting that
  import wrong substitutes a gamma overlap into a k-point stress, which is
  correct at `nkpts = 1` and wrong everywhere else — the same failure shape as
  18-06 Task 1's spin-summing error, and equally invisible on the obvious
  fixture.
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 4: the DFT+U strain terms</name>
  Port `_get_first_order_local_orbitals` (`krks_stress.py:336-362`) and
  `_hubbard_U_deriv1` (`krks_stress.py:363-403`, `kuks_stress.py:263-307`).

  These are the **strain** derivatives of the local orbitals; 18-08 Task 1
  built the **nuclear-coordinate** ones. Same Löwdin orthogonalisation, two
  different perturbations. Share whatever is genuinely shared (the eigenbasis
  solve of the Sylvester relation) and name the two entry points so they cannot
  be confused, exactly as 18-12 Task 4 requires for the two `ewald` derivatives.

  `_set_U` / `_make_minao_lo` / `reference_mol` are called from
  `pyscf_pbc_dft::kspu`, never reimplemented.

  Gate against `test_krks_stress.py:373 test_hubbard_U`.
</task>
